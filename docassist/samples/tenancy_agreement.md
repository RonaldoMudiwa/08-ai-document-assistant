# Tenancy Agreement

This is a made up example document for trying the program. The people, address and amounts are not real.

## The parties

This agreement is between the landlord, Mr Daniel Hart, and the tenant, Ms Amara Osei. The property is Flat 2, 14 Orchard Road, Ipswich.

## Rent

The rent is £950 per month. Rent is paid on the first day of each month by standing order. If rent is more than 14 days late, a late fee of £25 may be charged.

## Deposit

The deposit is £1,100. It is protected in a government approved tenancy deposit scheme within 30 days of being paid. The deposit is returned within 10 days of the end of the tenancy, minus any agreed deductions for damage or unpaid rent.

## Length of the tenancy

The tenancy is for 12 months, starting on 1 September. After that it carries on month by month until either side ends it.

## Ending the tenancy

The tenant must give at least one month's notice in writing to end the tenancy. The landlord must give at least two months' notice in writing.

## Repairs

The landlord is responsible for repairs to the structure, the boiler, the plumbing and the wiring. The tenant must report any repair needed as soon as possible. The tenant is responsible for replacing light bulbs and keeping the drains clear.

## Pets and smoking

Pets are not allowed without written permission from the landlord. Smoking is not allowed anywhere inside the property.

## Bills

The tenant pays council tax, gas, electricity, water and internet. The landlord pays the building insurance.
